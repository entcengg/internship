    How to apply

    1. Read the official page first

      Check the eligibility, the dates and the stipend there. If the official page differs from this site, trust the official page and tell the Internship Coordinator.

    2. Keep your papers ready

      A one-page résumé, your mark sheets up to the last semester, your college ID and a government photo ID cover most applications.

    3. Ask for the letter early

      ISRO, DRDO and most government organisations need an NOC or recommendation letter from the college. Ask the department well before the deadline, with the organisation's name and your internship dates.

    4. Apply only on the official site

      Government portals are free. Do not pay an agent or a website that promises a guaranteed internship. Programmes that charge a fee are marked “Fee-based” here.

    5. Tell the department

      Let the Internship Coordinator know when you apply and when you are selected, so the department can help with letters and approvals.

    Listings are run by the organisations named, not by the college. Confirm every detail on the official page before you apply. Programmes that charge a fee are labelled “Fee-based”.
