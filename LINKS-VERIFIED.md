## Removed since the first version

The nine AICTE shortcut links (by district, by stream, remote, closing this
week, C-DAC) are gone. Those pages fill in their results with a script, and
they showed "No postings" when opened, so they could not be confirmed. The
AICTE portal itself, the Government of Maharashtra page and the MathWorks page
stay because their content was readable.
